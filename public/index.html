Use MusicNuke with your own Spotify app

Spotify only lets each app have 5 approved users. You can get around that by creating your own free Spotify app and logging in through it. Takes about 5 minutes, and you only do it once.

You'll need Spotify Premium. Spotify requires it for anyone who creates an app.

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard

    Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard, log in with your normal Spotify account and accept the developer terms if asked.

  2. Create an app

    Click Create app and fill it in:

    • App name / description: anything, e.g. "My MusicNuke"
    • Redirect URI: paste this exactly, then click Add:
      …
    • Which API are you planning to use? tick Web API

    Agree to the terms and click Save.

  3. Add yourself as a user

    In your new app, open Settings → User Management and add your name and the email of your Spotify account, if it isn't listed already.

  4. Copy your Client ID

    In Settings → Basic Information, copy the Client ID and paste it below. You don't need the Client Secret, so never paste that anywhere.

    Your Client ID is saved in this browser, so next time it's one click.

Something went wrong?
  • "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid client": the Client ID is wrong. Copy it again from Basic Information.
  • "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI": the redirect URI on your app doesn't match exactly. Copy it from step 2 and make sure you clicked Add and Save.
  • "Your account isn't approved": add your Spotify email under User Management (step 3).
  • It stopped working later: check your Premium subscription is active.

What do you want to nuke?

Search for an artist or song. Add as many as you like.

    Tap Nuke on a chip to switch it to Keep. Kept songs and artists are protected, so you can nuke an artist but hold on to one song or a feature.
